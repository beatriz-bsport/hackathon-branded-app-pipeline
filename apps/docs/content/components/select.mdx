## Overview

Select replaces native `<select>` with a styled trigger and popover menu. It
supports grouping, icons, loading, and form submission via a hidden `name` input.
Use it for short to medium lists where search is unnecessary. For searchable or
multi-select lists, use [Autocomplete](/components/autocomplete).

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Group related items with title rows so staff can scan the popover." />
  <Do caption="Use controlled mode (`value` + `onChange`) when the selection drives other fields." />
  <Do caption="Pair `status='critical'` with actionable `errorText` after interaction or submit." />
  <Dont caption="Yes/no choices read more clearly on Toggle or Radio than Select." />
  <Dont caption="Empty `items` opens a popover with no choices — populate items or disable the field." />
</DoDont>

<Callout type="warning" title="Caution">

Very long flat lists are hard to scan. Group with title items or move to
Autocomplete when staff need search.

</Callout>

### Props

<PropsTable component="Select" />

Opens on trigger click or `Enter` / `Space`; closes on selection, `Escape`, or
focus leaving. `fullWidth` expands trigger and popover to container width.
