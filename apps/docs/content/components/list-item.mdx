## Overview

One row inside [List](/components/list). Primary content on the left — `title`,
avatar or icon, chips — metadata and actions on the right via `rightTitle` and
row buttons. Collection-level patterns live on the List page.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="`title` is the scan line; `rightTitle` for secondary meta." />
  <Do caption="Up to three chips per row." />
  <Do caption="`link` for navigation; `onItemClick` for selection without a route change." />
  <Dont caption="Row link plus competing action buttons — pick one interaction model." />
</DoDont>
