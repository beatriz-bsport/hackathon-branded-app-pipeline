## Punctuation

### Oxford comma

Use the Oxford comma in lists when items are distinct from each other.

> Packs, Subscriptions, Passes, and Gift Cards

You may also use it to separate two distinct words or actions in the same
sentence.

> Buy, edit, or delete your subscriptions.

### General punctuation

- Keep punctuation light in UI copy — avoid exclamation points and ellipses
  unless the locale guide says otherwise.
- Use sentence case for buttons, labels, and headings (`Save changes`, not
  `SAVE CHANGES`).
- One idea per sentence in instructional and error copy.

## Numbers

Write numbers in numerical form (`1`, `2`, `3`) rather than spelling them out
(`one`, `two`, `three`).

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Complete this 3-question survey to get started." />
  <Dont caption="Complete this three-question survey to get started." />
</DoDont>

Exception: spell out numbers at the start of a sentence in long-form content, or
when a locale style guide requires a different convention.

## Capitalisation

Capitalise proper nouns and bsport glossary terms consistently (`Member Area`,
`Gift Card`, `Subscription`). Do not capitalise common nouns for emphasis.
