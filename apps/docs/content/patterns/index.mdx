---
title: Patterns
description: Reusable UI patterns composed from Kaizen primitives.
category: patterns
---

Patterns describe how Kaizen primitives combine into recognisable
back-office flows — empty states, multi-step forms, list-detail layouts, and
the like. They sit one level above components and one level below product
features.

Pick a pattern from the sidebar. Pages are scaffolds to be filled in over time —
overview, specs, accessibility, and resources sub-pages are intentionally
omitted for now.
