The Kaizen component catalogue documents every primitive in the design system.
Each page follows the same structure — **Overview**, **Guidelines**, **Reference**,
**Accessibility**, and **Resources** — with live Storybook embeds where available.

<Callout type="warning" title="Accessibility and WCAG">

**Accessibility** sections and **WCAG** criteria on component pages describe target guidelines and expected behavior — not a guarantee that the implementation already meets them. Treat them as design and engineering intent; verify in Storybook and product before assuming compliance.

</Callout>

Browse by category below or use the sidebar. [Shared form guidelines](/components/shared-form-guidelines)
apply across all form controls.
