bsport ships in five languages. Voice stays consistent globally; these guides
capture how each locale expresses that voice — formality, addressing, and
grammar conventions that differ from English.

Use the central glossary for approved translations of platform terms before
writing or reviewing locale copy.

## German

### Tone and addressing

- Use informal **du** in lowercase — except at the start of a sentence — for a
  friendly, familiar tone.

> Du kannst dein Passwort hier ändern.

- Avoid bureaucratic or overly formal expressions.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Du musst…" />
  <Dont caption="Der Benutzer muss…" />
</DoDont>

- Keep error states direct, helpful, and calm.

> Das hat nicht geklappt. Versuch es bitte nochmal.

- Use short, confident instructions — `Profil speichern`.

### Grammar and style

- Active constructions; avoid long nested sentences.
- Present tense and consistent verb forms.
- Sentence case in interface text — `Zurück`, not `ZURÜCK`.
- Split complex messages into smaller sentences.

### Language

- Modern, everyday German — easy to read.
- Avoid idioms, slang, and outdated technical terms.
- Translate meaning, not literally — `Zeit auswählen` for "Select time".
- Follow glossary terminology.

### Accessibility

- One idea per sentence — `Wähle einen Termin aus`.
- Avoid complex grammar or context-dependent references.
- Keep structure consistent across screens.

## French

### Tone and addressing

- Use formal **vous** throughout — preferred in professional contexts.

> Vous pouvez modifier vos préférences ici.

- Avoid overly casual phrasing.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Veuillez cliquer ici." />
  <Dont caption="Salut, cliquez ici !" />
</DoDont>

- Keep error messages serious and respectful.

> Une erreur s'est produite. Veuillez réessayer.

- Provide clear, direct instructions — `Cliquez sur le bouton pour continuer.`
- Professional and trustworthy without sounding cold — `Votre réservation a bien été enregistrée.`

### Grammar and style

- Standard French grammar and punctuation.
- Active voice; avoid passive constructions.
- Clean punctuation — avoid exclamation points and ellipses.
- Sentence case for buttons and headings.
- Short, direct sentences with consistent verb tenses.

### Language

- Widely understood French — not regional or informal expressions.
- Avoid English borrowings unless standard in context.
- Precise action verbs — `Réserver`, `Confirmer`, `Annuler`.
- Clarity over literal word-for-word translation.
- Follow glossary terminology.

### Accessibility

- One clear instruction per sentence — `Réservez votre place`.
- Common action verbs; no metaphors or figurative language.
- Simple, familiar structure; each sentence understandable on its own.

## Italian

### Tone and addressing

- Use informal **tu** — friendly and natural for Italian users.

> Puoi aggiornare le impostazioni qui.

- Avoid formal or distant language.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Modifica le tue impostazioni qui." />
  <Dont caption="Gentile utente, La invitiamo a…" />
</DoDont>

- Light, calm, supportive feedback — `Qualcosa è andato storto. Riprova.`
- Encourage without over-explaining — `Tocca per continuare.`
- Everyday spoken phrasing — `Hai aggiornato il tuo profilo.`

### Grammar and style

- Present tense and active voice — `Seleziona un orario`, not passive equivalents.
- Minimal punctuation and capitalisation.
- Sentence case — `Prenota ora`, not `PRENOTA ORA`.
- Simple, direct sentence structure; consistent verb agreement.

### Language

- Clear standard Italian; avoid idioms, business jargon, and regionalisms.
- Direct functional verbs — `Conferma`, `Annulla`, `Prenota`.
- Clarity over literal translation.
- Follow glossary terminology.

### Accessibility

- One clear idea per sentence — `Aggiorna la tua password per continuare.`
- Familiar words for clear actions; no abstract expressions.
- Support skimming; every instruction stands alone.

## Spanish

### Tone and addressing

- Use informal **tú** — preferred by most Spanish users.

> Puedes cambiar tu contraseña aquí.

- Avoid formal or impersonal language.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Escribe tu nombre de usuario." />
  <Dont caption="Por favor, introduzca su nombre de usuario." />
</DoDont>

- Friendly, calm system feedback — `Todo listo. Ya puedes empezar.`
- Short, inviting CTAs — `Reserva tu plaza` instead of `Haz clic para reservar`.
- Natural conversational phrasing — `Has actualizado tu información.`

### Grammar and style

- Active voice and present tense — `Selecciona una opción`.
- Light punctuation and minimal capitalisation.
- Sentence case — `Cancelar`, not `CANCELAR`.
- Consistent sentence patterns.

### Language

- Neutral Spanish for wide understanding.
- Avoid idioms, regionalisms, and overly technical terms.
- Action verbs that match the task — `Guardar`, `Editar`, `Finalizar`.
- Translate by meaning and context.
- Follow glossary terminology.

### Accessibility

- One action or instruction per sentence — `Actualiza tu perfil antes de continuar.`
- Short sentences with clear intent; no figurative language.
- Verbs that describe what happens next; text understandable at a glance.

## Dutch

### Tone and addressing

- Use informal **je/jij** — natural and friendly.

> Je kunt je profiel hier aanpassen.

- Avoid very formal or old-fashioned phrasing.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Vul het formulier in." />
  <Dont caption="Gelieve het formulier in te vullen." />
</DoDont>

- Calm, direct error messages — `Er is iets misgegaan. Probeer het opnieuw.`
- Short, polite, action-focused — `Je les is toegevoegd.`
- Avoid overly enthusiastic tone.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Klaar. Je wijziging is opgeslagen." />
  <Dont caption="Fantastisch! Je hebt het gedaan!" />
</DoDont>

### Grammar and style

- Active sentences and simple verb forms — `Selecteer een tijd`.
- Avoid long or complex structures.
- Sentence case — `Annuleren`, not `ANNULEREN`.
- Clean, consistent punctuation; consistent verb tenses.

### Language

- Modern, everyday Dutch.
- Avoid idioms, formal business terms, and slang.
- Clear descriptive action words — `Bevestigen` for Confirm, `Boeken` for Book.
- Translate meaning, not just words.
- Follow glossary terminology.

### Accessibility

- One clear idea per sentence — `Wijzig je wachtwoord om verder te gaan.`
- Familiar verbs; avoid vague expressions.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Kies een tijdslot." />
  <Dont caption="Doe wat nodig is." />
</DoDont>

- Each sentence clear on its own; instructions short and scannable.
