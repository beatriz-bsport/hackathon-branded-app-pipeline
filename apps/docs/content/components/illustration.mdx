## Overview

Illustration renders curated spot artwork from the Kaizen illustration set. Unlike
[Icon](/components/icon), illustrations are expressive and larger — they support
empty states, error fallbacks, and onboarding moments where a neutral icon would
feel too sparse.

### Use cases

- **Empty lists** — no members, passes, or templates yet in Studio Manager tables.
- **Error fallback** — paired with [Error fallback](/components/error-fallback) artwork slots.
- **Onboarding panels** — first-run education beside [Title](/components/title) and CTA buttons.
- **Success moments** — completion screens after imports or setup wizards.
- **Marketing-style modals** — hero art above form content when emotion helps adoption.

## Guidelines

- **One illustration per view** — a single focal artwork; multiple compete for attention.
- **Match name to scenario** — pick illustrations designed for the empty or error case.
- **Size to layout** — use documented size tokens; avoid stretching beyond max bounds.
- **Always pair with copy** — illustration supports the message; Title and Body carry meaning.
- **Decorative by default** — mark as decorative unless it is the only content on screen.

### Best practices

#### General

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Center illustration above title and body in empty states with consistent vertical rhythm." />
  <Do caption="Choose artwork that matches the scenario — empty inbox vs connection error." />

### Content rules

Headlines stay short; body explains what happened and what to do next:

> No email templates yet

> Create a template to contact members faster.

## Accessibility

### ARIA roles & attributes

Follow Storybook accessibility examples: decorative illustrations should use
`aria-hidden="true"` when adjacent text conveys the message.

### Keyboard navigation

<Accessibility
  keyboard={[{ keys: ["Tab"], description: "Illustration is not focusable." }]}
/>

### Screen reader behavior

Do not rely on illustration alone to communicate errors or empty states — visible
and programmatic headings must repeat the message.

### Focus management

Move focus to the primary action button after empty-state render when it is the
main exit from the dead end.

### WCAG criteria

- **1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)** — text paired with illustration still must meet contrast.
- **4.1.2 Name, Role, Value** — headings label the state, not the artwork.
