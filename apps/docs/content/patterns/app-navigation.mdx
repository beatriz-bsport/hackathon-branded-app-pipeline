## Overview

The left sidebar owns global wayfinding; the top bar owns feature-internal navigation.

The _left sidebar owns global wayfinding_ across the entire Back Office, while the _top bar owns feature-internal wayfinding_ that adapts to whichever module is currently active. This creates a clear spatial contract so users always know which layer of the system they are interacting with.

This is the persistent backbone of the application. It answers the question _"Where can I go in the entire Back Office?"_ and should remain visible (or easily collapsible) across all screens.

### What belongs here

- _Feature Modules_: High-level product areas such as _Scheduling_, _Profiles_, _Payments_, _Marketing_, or _Analytics_
- _Cross-Cutting tools_: Utilities that span all features, such as _Global Search_, _Notifications…_
- _Quick Actions / Shortcuts_: Frequently used creation entry points like Sales, _Create Booking(idea)_ or _Add Member_
- _System & Utility Items_: _Settings_, _Help Center_, _Account/Profile_, and _Logout_ — visually de-emphasized and grouped at the bottom of the sidebar

### Why this works

Side navigation scales comfortably to 10–20+ items and supports logical grouping without forcing hierarchy into cramped dropdowns. Eye-tracking research also shows that left navigation reduces cursor movement and selection errors compared to top navigation alone.

### Top Bar: Feature-internal navigation

This bar is scoped to the current feature and answers the question _"What can I do inside this feature?"_ It should update dynamically based on which sidebar item is active.

### What belongs here

- _Sub-Feature Tabs_: Sections within the active module. For example, inside _Members_ you might find _Related passes_, Object overview, _Import/Export, etc_
- _Contextual Actions_: Primary and secondary actions tied to the current view, such as _Add Member_, _Export CSV_, or _Apply Filters_
- _Breadcrumbs_: Hierarchical wayfinding when a feature has nested pages, helping users orient themselves without overloading the sidebar
- _Page Title & Status Badges_: The current page name plus optional indicators like _Listed_

## Guidelines

### `CONSIDERING: Double top-navigation`

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- _Duplicating hierarchy_: If _Clients_ is a top-level sidebar item, its sub-sections (_Profiles, Tags_) should live only in the top bar, not repeat in both locations
- _Deep nesting in the sidebar_: Avoid multi-level flyouts in the left bar; diagonal cursor movement makes them error-prone and they clutter the global view
- _Global actions in the top bar_: Actions like switching organizations or account settings belong in the sidebar; placing them in the top bar falsely implies they are scoped to the current feature
